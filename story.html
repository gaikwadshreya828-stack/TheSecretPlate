Our Story

A plate full of stories

It begins before sunrise, at the harbours along the Maharashtra coast. The fishermen bring in their catch — surmai, prawns, calamari — still glistening with seawater. We're there to choose the best, every single morning.

The Secret Plate was born from a simple idea: that the freshest seafood, treated with respect and seasoned with the bold coastal flavours of the Konkan, deserves to be plated with intention. Not hidden behind heavy sauces. Not overcooked. Just the catch, the spice, and the fire.

Our kitchen is where tradition meets restraint. We grind our masalas daily. We use ghee, kokum, coconut and curry leaves — the building blocks of coastal cooking — but we measure, we taste, and we plate with the precision of a chef who knows that a guest's first impression is visual.

Every dish tells a story. The prawn ghee roast carries the warmth of Mangalorean kitchens. The tawa surmai is a Mumbai street-side memory, elevated. The coastal fish curry is a recipe that travelled from a grandmother's hand to ours, and now — we hope — to your table.
