Welcome

Not just seafood.
A plate full of stories.

Every dish at The Secret Plate begins long before it reaches your table. We source the freshest catch from the Maharashtra coast each morning, layer it with coastal spices and traditional technique, and plate it with a modern eye.

From the smoky char of our tawa-grilled surmai to the slow-simmered depth of our coastal fish curry, each plate carries the warmth of a Konkan kitchen and the precision of a chef who cares.
